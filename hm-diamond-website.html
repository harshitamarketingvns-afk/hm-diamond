Know Your Gold
Gold Purity Guide
Understanding karatage helps you choose the right jewellery for every occasion.
9
Karat
9 Karat Gold — 37.5% Pure
375 parts per thousand gold. Most durable, scratch-resistant. Ideal for everyday fashion jewellery and lightweight designs. Lower price point, maximum durability.
14
Karat
14 Karat Gold — 58.5% Pure
585 parts per thousand gold. Perfect balance of purity and strength. Most popular worldwide for diamond jewellery settings — holds stones securely. Great for rings and bracelets.
18
Karat
18 Karat Gold — 75% Pure
750 parts per thousand gold. Premium quality, richer colour, luxurious feel. The preferred choice for high-end diamond jewellery, bridal sets, and heirloom pieces. HM Diamond's most popular grade.
